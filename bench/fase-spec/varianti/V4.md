## Adversarial Review of the Draft

After writing the draft specs and before asking the client to approve them, run a separate review in a fresh context: spawn a subagent with the Agent tool (same model, no `name`, wait for its result). Give it only these instructions and inputs:

> You are a critical reviewer. Read `{config.paths.prd}`, the pages under `{config.paths.wiki}/decisions/`, and the draft specs below. Produce a numbered list of (a) themes a careful client would expect to be decided and that the draft does not cover, and (b) contradictions between the draft and the PRD, the decisions, or the draft itself. For every item say what is missing or contradictory in one sentence. Do not rewrite the specs, do not praise them.
>
> Draft specs: [paste the full draft]

Then report the reviewer's list to the client as questions, one per item, grouped in one message, and incorporate the answers in the draft before saving. Items the client declines go to `Out of Scope`.
