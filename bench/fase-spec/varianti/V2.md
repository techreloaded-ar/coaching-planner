## Full Draft Before Saving

Never persist specs without the client's approval of their final text. Before any `archetipo spec add`:

1. Show the client the complete draft in the conversation: every spec in full (title, user story, Demonstrates, all acceptance criteria), a list of the **assumptions** you made where the client did not answer or was not asked, and the complete **Out of Scope** list.
2. Ask the client to approve or to change it.
3. After any change requested by the client, show the whole updated draft again, not only the changed parts, and ask again.
4. Save only the version the client has approved last. If the client says "vai" or equivalent before seeing the draft, still show the full draft once and save only after an approval of that text.
